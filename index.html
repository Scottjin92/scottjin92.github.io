About

Behind the lens

I'm Roger Yue — a drone operator and cinematographer based in Vancouver. I've spent years on commercial, music-video, and documentary sets, and these days I work as a gun for hire: you bring the project, I bring the gear and the shots.

FAQ

Good to know

Who is Roger Yue?

Roger Yue is a Vancouver-based aerial drone operator and cinematographer, and the founder of Creative Research Ltd. — a Transport Canada RPAS Advanced Operations–certified, fully insured drone pilot available for hire across Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland.

What areas do you serve?

Vancouver and the Lower Mainland — including North and West Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, and the North Shore. Available to travel farther on request.

Are you a licensed and insured drone pilot?

Yes — Transport Canada RPAS Advanced Operations certified and fully insured.

Do you handle editing and post-production?

No. I'm a capture-only operator — I shoot the footage and hand it off for your team to edit. No pre-production, no post.

What do you shoot?

Aerial and drone cinematography, real estate and property, commercials and brand films, music videos, and events, plus ground camera operation. Booked by the day or half-day.

How do I book a shoot?

Email scottjin92@gmail.com or use the form below with your shoot date, location, and what you need captured. I'll reply with availability and a day rate.
